## 0. Why we build this (business case)

The product exists for professional creators — photographers, studios, and
content teams — who use AI-assisted editing on their own work: retouching,
cleanup, and privacy edits such as blurring confidential information out of a
scene. Those edits are legitimate, but the moment an AI-assisted edit is
applied, generic AI detectors can misclassify the creator's own photograph as
machine-generated content. For a professional, that misclassification has real
consequences: it can strip attribution and ownership credit from work the
creator authored, and it can block distribution on platforms that auto-screen
for AI content.

The core value proposition: **the creator did the work; the AI was a tool.**
The finished image should read as the creator's photograph — not as an AI
output — so the creator retains credit, ownership, and distribution access.
That is the market problem this product solves, and it is why both of the
requirements in §1 are non-negotiable.

## 1. Product context (what the software does)

Remarkee Max processes images after AI-assisted editing so the delivered files
are not flagged as AI-generated by detectors. The product has two hard
requirements, in priority order:

1. **Detection eligibility** (the product's reason to exist): delivered output
   must satisfy ai ≤ 0.45, flux-family ≤ 0.30, deepfake ≤ 0.10 at real
   vendors. A beautiful image that gets flagged is a failed product.
2. **Visual quality** (why creators pay): sharp, natural, premium,
   product-content-grade images.

Every experiment is pre-registered: pass/fail numbers frozen before first light,
sealed paired cells (same image, same seed, one variable), hash-verified
provenance, free simulated detector grades first, real vendor spend only after
screening passes (40-call session cap, hard).

## 2. The pipeline (measured reality)

Per image: O0 source → O1 Qwen wash (regenerative; the only proven breaker of
the generator watermark — and when it fails it re-stamps a different
fingerprint; we measured 99.9→99.9 swaps) → O2 coherent-camera re-life
(non-generative: resample ≤1250 + simulated lens/sensor/demosaic/sharpen) →
O3 stage-1 JPEG q92 → O4 Quality Finish (selective restoration) → O5 delivery
JPEG + EXIF. The detector gate is probed throughout and routes per image.
