# MASTER PROMPT — 4D SYSTEMS AUDIT (FRESH-CHAT, SANDBOXED, UNBIASED)

Role: independent systems architect. You are being asked for a **full, unbiased,
first-principles audit** of the current image pipeline and its 4D quality
program — not to continue it, but to question it. You may conclude the current
architecture is right, partially wrong, or should be replaced.

Two copies of this prompt are being issued in two separate fresh chats (builder
C88 and Consultant D). Work **independently**; do not coordinate; the owner
cross-reads both responses.

## 0. Honest context (read first)

The product takes AI-generated images and delivers reworked images that cannot
be flagged as AI-generated by detectors. Business truth: the creator did the
creative work; the AI was a tool; delivery images are often photos of people
with privacy redaction. Professional photographers whose own camera photos get
misclassified as AI are the legitimate use case. Nothing about this analysis
changes that product truth.

## 1. Sandbox rules (hard)

- Analysis ONLY. No commit, no deploy, no RunPod, no Supabase, no live cells,
  no grading calls, no vendor calls, no file writes beyond a returned
  document. You may read code and data in the workspace for evidence.
- No post-hoc changes to frozen numbers, gates, or evidence pins. If you
  believe a frozen number is wrong, say so explicitly as a finding — do not
  silently use a different one.
- Label every claim: measured fact, code-derived fact, model estimate, or
  opinion. No sycophancy. Challenge the premises, including the ones the
  owner holds (e.g., "rephotography is the unlock").
- Answer the standing questions directly. "I don't know yet" is an acceptable
  answer where the only honest path is a measurement.

## 2. System under audit (measured facts only)

Pipeline: `O0 source → O1 generative wash → OR resample → O2 camera ladder →
O3 stage-1 JPEG (q92 4:2:0) → O4 Quality Finish → O5 delivered (QF q97 4:4:4,
finalize pass-through)`. Delivery resolution: ≤1250 px long edge. Naturalization
module exists in the codebase but is **off** on the delivery path (`output_mode
"stripped"`).

Measured band-energy ratios vs source (mean over 12 archived cells):
- H1 (mid-band): O1 **0.938** → O2 **0.484** → O5 **0.362**
- H0 (fine): O1 **0.608** → O2 **0.290** → O5 **0.126**
- H2 (coarse): O5 **0.755**
- Camera-ladder-only H1 loss: **0.543185**; resample-only loss: **0.000000**
  (geometry-normalized comparison).
- Wash output retains 0.938 of source H1, but destroys source correspondence:
  NCC ≥ 0.90 passes on 0.023–0.126% of pixels (0.6–1.55M rejects per cell);
  cross-scale agreement ~0%.

Program history: two commissioned rounds failed. 4D-CAM-1 (camera PSF
modulation) too weak. 4D-1a (direct OR→O2 transfer) never fired — wash
destroyed correspondence, so eligibility gates passed nowhere; all 12 candidate
cells were byte-identical to control. Current 4D-1b (OR→O2 H1-only loss-
constrained restore, dose 25% of positive loss) is in replay; replay harness
proved stage-1 fidelity byte-exact (O3 12/12) but O4/O5 differ ±1/±7 LSB from
archives, diagnosed as float32 environment difference (archived worker = Linux
conda numpy; replays = macOS pip numpy). Recovery ceiling under perfect local
correlation: `r' = (0.75√r + 0.25)²`, at measured r = 0.456815 → **21.3%** of
the camera-stage loss; at the planned 25% dose the delivered H1/source would
move from 0.362 to ≈0.46 against a frozen floor gate of 0.420.

Test regime: staged evidence funnel (replay → live 24-cell MOCK screen →
human panel → single-vendor Hive leg, 12 calls). MOCK is screening only; no
real detector has ever scored any candidate.

## 3. The audit task

### A. First principles (≤1 page)
Decompose the objective mathematically: what must be true of an image's
statistics for current detectors to flag it; what must be true for it to be a
high-quality natural photo; state precisely where the two objectives are
opposed and where they are not. Name the measurable quantities that define
"quality" and "naturalness" independently of detector scores.

### B. Per-stage flow audit
For each stage O0→O5 (plus the off-path naturalization module): what it buys,
what it costs (with the measured numbers above), weakest link, and a verdict —
keep as-is / tune / replace / delete. Say explicitly which stage is the
binding constraint on quality and which on detection.

### C. SWOT of the current architecture
Compact; evidence-linked, not generic.

### D. Option space — derived independently
Generate the widest credible option set across the full architecture space,
including (but not limited to): adaptive stage strengths with detector-in-the-
loop, learned image-to-image naturalization, real vs simulated rephotography
variants, wash-depth policy, ordering permutations, resolution policy, and
multi-pass or iterative structures. Do NOT restrict yourself to options the
current program is considering. For each option: expected quality effect,
expected detector risk, build cost, freeze impact, and whether it can be
measured in a cheap archived-cell replay.

### E. Optimal positioning and best-moves strategy chain
Recommend a sequence of the next 2–3 moves with explicit stop conditions and
decision criteria (e.g., under what measured result we continue 4D-1b, pivot,
or kill it). Prioritize the cheapest falsifying measurement first.

### F. Standing questions — answer directly
1. Is rephotography (real or simulated) the quality unlock, the quality
   destroyer, or neither?
2. Why is this taking long — structural reasons, not process excuses?
3. Honest probability of the end product (unflagable today + visually natural),
   and what would raise it fastest.

## 4. Deliverable format

Single document, sections A–F, signed, dated. Keep A–C tight (one page each
max); D and E are the core — spend your length there. Every number carries a
label (measured / code-derived / estimate / opinion). End with a one-paragraph
"if I owned this" verdict.

The owner forwards both audits to the master engineer; the two responses will
be cross-read for agreement, disagreement, and blind spots.
